Two edits of one wedding

Cinematic film or traditional video?

One is for watching with friends; the other is for the family archive. Most couples choose both, and the same crew can shoot for both edits.

Cinematic wedding film

What it is
A highlight film edited like a short movie, with music, vows and speeches woven across all your events.
How it feels
Emotional and paced. It follows the story of the wedding rather than the order of the schedule.

Traditional videography

What it is
A complete edited video of each event, so the ceremonies can be watched in full in years to come.
How it feels
Thorough and clear. Every ritual, every speech and every family member on stage.
You receive
One edited traditional video for each covered event.

How we make the film

We listen first, then film.

Sound carries a wedding film: a father's speech, the vows, your friends' toast. We record clean audio where it matters, and build the edit around those voices.

On the day, we film from several angles with movement and detail, without directing every moment. The cinematic film then weaves every event into one story instead of a string of separate clips.

  1. Plan

    We go through each event, the music you love and the moments that matter.

  2. Film

    Multi-angle coverage, clean audio for vows and speeches, detail and atmosphere.

  3. Edit

    A cinematic highlight film, plus a traditional video for each event.

Wedding film questions

Do we need both a cinematic film and traditional video?

The cinematic film is the one you will share and rewatch. Traditional video keeps the full ceremonies, which parents and grandparents often value most. If you choose one, we will explain what you give up.

Can we choose the music?

Yes. Share songs you love and we will suggest what works for the edit.

Will the film crew get in the way of the ceremony?

We agree positions with you and your priest, qazi or parish beforehand, and keep a respectful distance during the most important moments.

How long does the edit take?

Traditional videos come after each event is edited; the cinematic film follows once the full wedding edit is complete.
