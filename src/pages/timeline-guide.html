A Mumbai wedding timeline that leaves room for photographs

A calmer schedule makes room for photographs without making the wedding feel like a photoshoot.

By Shaadi Films · 14 October 2026 · 3 min read

In this guide

  • Start with what cannot move
  • Plan journeys as part of the day
  • Protect a few short portrait windows
  • Give the team a useful brief

Start with what cannot move

Write down the ceremony time, venue access, meal service and any agreed finish time before scheduling portraits. Ask the people leading the ceremony which moments must happen at a fixed time and which can move. The photography plan should fit around those commitments. Share the same schedule with your planner, makeup artist and photo and film teams so that changes reach everyone.

Plan journeys as part of the day

If getting ready, the ceremony and the reception are in different places, give each transfer its own place in the schedule. In Mumbai, a plan that assumes every journey will run perfectly leaves little room for a relaxed portrait. Ask your planner or family coordinator to check the route and build in a buffer appropriate to the actual date and journey. Keep a small kit with touch-up essentials and the things you need for the next event.

Protect a few short portrait windows

A short, well-prepared session is often easier to fit into a wedding than one long shoot. Discuss a couple portrait before guests arrive, family groups after the ceremony and a few frames together before the reception. Share a named list of essential family groups and ask one person from each family to gather people. This helps the photographer stay with the couple instead of searching for relatives.

Give the team a useful brief

Share the final venues, contact numbers for the day, ceremony order and the moments that matter most. Include any private rituals or people who should not be photographed. If the schedule changes, tell the photo and film lead directly. Ask the team to explain which photographs become difficult if preparation runs late, so you can make a conscious choice rather than rush every part of the day.
