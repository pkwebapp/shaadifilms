From enquiry to album

Six steps, no surprises.

  1. Tell us about the wedding

    Dates, venues, events and what you have in mind. A rough plan is enough to start.

  2. Get a tailored proposal

    We suggest coverage and team size for each event, with deliverables listed clearly.

  3. Block your date

    A booking amount confirms the date and is adjusted against the total.

  4. Plan the day together

    We go through the order of ceremonies, family lists for group photos, and timings.

  5. We cover your events

    Photographers and filmmakers at each event as agreed, working to one plan.

  6. Edit, deliver, print

    Edited photos and films, then album design after you approve your selection.

Tailored coverage

A proposal, not a price list.

A three-event family wedding and a five-event wedding across two homes need very different teams. So we quote for your wedding rather than asking you to fit a package.

Guest experience and photo delivery

Guests find their own photos.

Instead of scrolling through thousands of pictures, guests take a selfie and see only the photos they appear in.

  1. Scan

    Guests scan the QR code at the QR booth, or on a table card, with their phone camera.

  2. Take a selfie

    The gallery, hosted on PIK Connect, matches their face to event photos.

  3. Save and share

    Guests download their photos.

Face-scan delivery is provided through PIK Connect, a separate photo-delivery platform.
